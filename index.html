Features

Explore different features

Every feature is designed to get out of your way.

Scrolling capture

Capture entire pages, long documents, or infinite scroll feeds in a single clean screenshot. No more stitching images together.

Annotation tools

A focused set of tools — arrows, callouts, blur, and text — designed to communicate clearly without the overhead of a full design app.

Instant shareable links

Every capture gets its own link the moment it's taken. Copy, paste, done. No account required for the recipient.

Enjoy numerous benefits

Vertex makes things 10x easier in your everyday workflow.

Custom shortcuts

Bind any capture mode to your own keyboard shortcut. Vertex works around your workflow, not the other way around.

Clipboard sync

Every capture lands in your clipboard automatically. Paste directly into Slack, Notion, email, anywhere.

All your captures are optionally backed up and accessible from any device. Never lose a screenshot again.

Markup history

Every annotation is non-destructive. Go back, edit, or remove markings at any time without touching the original.

Privacy controls

Works seamlessly across multiple monitors. Capture from any display without changing your setup.
